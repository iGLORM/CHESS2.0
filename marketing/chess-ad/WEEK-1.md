# Week 1 on social: posting plan

Seven vertical reels (1080×1920, 20–25 s, mastered to −14 LUFS) with a cover each and a 4:5 feed image.
Rendered files: `out/week-1/DayN-*.mp4`, `DayN-*-cover.png`, `DayN-*-post-4x5.png`.
Sources: the Studio's **Social** folder (`src/social/`).

Every day, post the same video to **TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts**. Use the cover as the thumbnail.
Post at about **19:00** local time. Spend the first hour after posting replying to every comment.
