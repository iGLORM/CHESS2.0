# Trailer revision — research and edit decisions

The first cut relied on five long text-led scenes. This revision has 25 shots in 32 seconds, arranged on a 150 BPM grid (one beat = 12 frames at 30 fps).

## Research applied

- [Derek Lieu: How to Hook the Audience and How Quickly to Do It](https://www.derek-lieu.com/blog/2022/10/24/how-to-hook-the-audience-and-how-quickly-to-do-it). Establish the familiar genre, then reveal the unusual mechanic early. Applied: chess → threatened knight → defensive challenge → surviving knight, all in the first eight seconds.
- [Derek Lieu: How to Think About Pacing in a Trailer](https://www.derek-lieu.com/blog/2020/11/16/how-to-think-about-pacing-in-a-trailer). Vary shot lengths and intensity; give sections distinct jobs. Applied: 0.8-second minigame cuts, a slower map section, a final acceleration, and a readable 4.8-second end scene.
- [Derek Lieu: Basic Guide to Trailer Sound Editing](https://www.derek-lieu.com/blog/2020/11/24/basic-guide-to-trailer-sound-editing). Give music and sound effects room to lead, rather than competing constantly. Applied: tape-stop pocket before the drop, a saved-piece chime, short transition whooshes, and a rising cue into the title chord.
- [Steamworks: Trailers](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/trailer). Show gameplay early and make the concept understandable without audio. Applied: actual game captures dominate the edit; the capture/challenge/save sequence has short on-screen captions.

No footage or music was copied from reference trailers. The advice informed the edit; it is not a prediction of sales results.

## Structure

| Time | Purpose | Editing and sound |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3.2s | Threatened piece, then “Not yet” | Board push-in, target marker, snap close-up, tape stop |
| 3.2–8s | Show the capture defense mechanic | Runner challenge, jump to the finish, real defended board; bass drop and save arpeggio |
| 8–16s | Show the variety | Six short gameplay shots, moving triple view, kinetic verbs; full drum groove |
| 16–22.4s | Story and guardians | Moving world map, restoration event, parallax guardian entrances; musical contrast |
| 22.4–27.2s | Final escalation | Outthink / Outplay / Outlast montage; denser percussion and riser |
| 27.2–32s | Remember the game and act | Letters assemble into logo over moving gameplay, persistent URL; resolved chord |

The capture setup uses a legal bishop capture attempt on a white knight. The recorded minigame is genuinely won by the built-in bot; `defended: true` was confirmed before using the surviving-board shot.

The CTA stays `PLAY CHESS 2.0` / `game.altobolt.com` until a confirmed purchase destination is supplied. No unconfirmed price, launch status, store logo, review score, or award is shown.
