# Original native pixel bosses

These six assets are original procedural drawings, not generated-image edits or resized illustrations. Their exact reproducible source is `../../characters/generate_pixel_bosses.py`.

Design brief: create six distinct readable chess-inspired boss silhouettes on 96 by 112 transparent canvases, with crisp pixel clusters, dark outlines, limited palettes, and no anti-aliasing. Preserve empty margins, show every character in full, and use binary alpha (0 or 255).

- Moss Pawn Warden: round mushroom cap, moss cloak, walking staff and sturdy feet.
- Tide Knight Captain: horse helmet, crest, shield and upright lance.
- Moon Bishop Seer: split bishop mitre, floating robe, crescent staff.
- Brass Rook Forgemaster: squat automaton, battlement crown, oversized fists.
- Frost Queen Regent: crystalline crown and wings, long icy gown.
- Ember King Anvil: cross crown, flame-colored beard, forge hammer and cape.

All names and designs are exploratory candidates, not additions to the game's story or runtime.
